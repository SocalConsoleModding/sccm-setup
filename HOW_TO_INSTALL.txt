IMPORTANT
  Use an SD card reader or Hekate's SD mount to copy files.
  Copying via MTP will take hours.

INSTALLATION

  1. Copy the app:
       /switch/tico.nro  ->  sdmc:/switch/tico.nro
                         or  sdmc:/switch/tico/tico.nro

  2. Copy the data folder:
       /tico  ->  sdmc:/tico
